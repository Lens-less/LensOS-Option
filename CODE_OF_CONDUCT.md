# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe, and equitable for everyone.
We respect the dignity, rights, and contributions of all participants regardless
of identity, background, ability, experience, education, or other status. The
same privileges of participation extend to everyone who participates in good
faith and follows this Code of Conduct.

## Encouraged Behaviors

We recognize that words and actions may be understood differently across
cultures, backgrounds, and native languages. We therefore agree to:

1. Respect the purpose of the project and its community spaces.
2. Engage kindly and honestly with others.
3. Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
4. Take responsibility for our actions and contributions.
5. Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
6. Repair harm when it occurs.
7. Act in ways that sustain the well-being of the community.

## Restricted Behaviors

The following behaviors, including threats or promotion of them, violate this
Code of Conduct:

1. Harassment, including violating clearly expressed boundaries or continuing
   unwanted personal attention after a request to stop.
2. Character attacks, insults, demeaning comments, or pejorative statements
   directed at an individual or group.
3. Stereotyping or discrimination based on identity or personal traits.
4. Sexualized behavior or attention inappropriate to the community context.
5. Sharing or acting on another person's private information without permission.
6. Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm.
7. Impersonation, evading enforcement, or otherwise presenting a misleading
   identity.
8. Failing to credit sources for contributed content.
9. Unsolicited marketing or commercial promotion outside community norms.
10. Other behavior that threatens the safety or well-being of the community.

## Reporting an Issue

Report possible violations privately through
[GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/Lens-less/LensOS-Option/security/advisories/new)
and begin the report with `Code of Conduct report`. Do not include sensitive
details in a public issue. If that private form is unavailable, ask a maintainer
for a private reporting channel without disclosing incident details publicly.

Project moderators will review reports promptly and fairly, prioritize the
safety and confidentiality of everyone involved, and keep enforcement actions
private except where a mutually agreed resolution calls for a community notice.

## Addressing and Repairing Harm

If project moderators determine that this Code of Conduct was violated, they may
use the following enforcement ladder. More serious incidents may skip earlier
steps.

1. **Warning**
   - Event: a single violation or related series of incidents.
   - Consequence: a private written warning.
   - Repair may include an apology, acknowledgement of responsibility, and
     clarification of expectations.
2. **Temporarily limited activities**
   - Event: a repeated violation after a warning, or a more serious first
     violation.
   - Consequence: a private warning and time-limited cooldown applying to
     specified spaces or interactions.
   - Repair may include reflection, apology, and a thoughtful return to the
     community after the cooldown.
3. **Temporary suspension**
   - Event: a repeated pattern that warnings did not resolve, or one serious
     violation.
   - Consequence: temporary removal from community spaces, with conditions for
     return.
   - Repair includes respecting the suspension and meeting all return
     conditions.
4. **Permanent ban**
   - Event: a severe violation or a pattern that other measures did not resolve.
   - Consequence: permanent removal from community spaces and communication
     channels.

This ladder is guidance and does not prevent moderators from exercising judgment
to protect the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when someone officially
represents the project in public or private spaces.
