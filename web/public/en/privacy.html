Privacy / Public static pages

Privacy policy

The public site is static. It does not set accounts, ship third-party trackers, or collect user profiles.

What we do not collect

  • No cookies.
  • No login or user accounts.
  • No third-party analytics, ads, or tracking scripts.
  • No third-party fonts, images, or embeds loaded on purpose.

Hosting logs

A static host may still record ordinary HTTP metadata such as IP address, user agent, request path, and timestamp according to the host's own policy. Those logs are controlled by the host, not by the front end.

Local companion

This page describes the public static site only. If a local Chrome companion is enabled, it operates only on loopback and is outside the public site's tracking boundary.
